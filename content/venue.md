The workshop takes place at the **NSF NCAR Mesa Laboratory**, 1850 Table Mesa Drive, Boulder, Colorado ([map](https://www.openstreetmap.org/search?query=NCAR%20Mesa%20Laboratory%20Boulder)).

The Mesa Laboratory sits at approximately 1,860 m (6,100 ft) above sea level at the base of the Flatirons. Visitors arriving from low elevation should stay well hydrated and allow for reduced exertion during the first day.

## Getting there

Most participants will fly into **Denver International Airport (DEN)**, about 70 km from Boulder. Ground transportation and parking details will be posted here.
