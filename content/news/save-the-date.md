The workshop on **Emulated and Hybrid ML-Physics Earth System Models** will take place on 12–14 May 2027 at the NSF NCAR Mesa Laboratory in Boulder, Colorado. The meeting will convene hybrid model developers, climate emulator developers, and researchers working at their interface to define a staged path toward trustworthy coupled hybrid models and emulators.

The call for contributions, registration, and the detailed program will be announced on this site.
