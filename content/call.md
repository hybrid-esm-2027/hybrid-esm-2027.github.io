## Scope

We welcome contributed talks and posters across three threads:

- **Hybrid ML-physics models** — ML parameterizations, hybrid CAM/CESM components, learned closures, stochastic parameterizations, differentiable or corrected physics, and process-constrained ML.
- **Climate emulators** — whole-atmosphere, ocean, and coupled emulators; response emulators; surrogate large ensembles; and fast forced-response or scenario tools.
- **Shared coupling and evaluation** — coupler interfaces, conservation, low-order tests, idealized benchmarks, online diagnostics, uncertainty quantification, software, intercomparison, and governance.

## Readiness self-assessment

Each submission should state the readiness level of the system it describes, from **R0** (conceptual or offline prototype) to **R6** (multi-decadal / centennial coupled response), using the [workshop readiness scale](../themes/#readiness-scale). Early-stage work (R0–R2) is explicitly welcome; the scale exists to make claims comparable, not to filter contributions.
